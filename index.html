WATCHGRID

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 21, 2026

Developer: Solidcrafts

Summary: Watchgrid does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage metrics, device identifiers, or tracking information whatsoever. Your privacy is 100% respected.

1. Information We Collect

Watchgrid collects zero personal data.

  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not integrate any third-party tracking, profiling, or analytical SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, or Google Analytics).
  • No Account Registration: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email address to use Watchgrid.
  • No Location or Sensor Data: The application does not access your GPS location, biometric sensors, microphone, or camera.

2. On-Device Functionality & Permissions

Watchgrid operates strictly on your local devices:

  • Watch Face Rendering: Our watch faces utilize the native Google/Samsung Watch Face Format (WFF) declarative XML standard. The watch faces render time, dates, and graphics locally on the watch hardware without network interaction.
  • Phone-to-Watch Bluetooth Sync: The companion application uses the Google Wearable DataLayer API strictly to transfer user preferences (such as selected timepiece settings) locally over Bluetooth between your paired Android phone and Wear OS smartwatch. No data leaves your personal devices.

3. Third-Party Services & Payments

All financial transactions and in-app purchases are handled directly and securely by Google Play. Solidcrafts has no access to your credit card information, bank details, or billing address.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with an updated effective date.
